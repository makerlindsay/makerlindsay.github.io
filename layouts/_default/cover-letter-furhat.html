Dear Furhat team,

When a robot responds to the person who builds or programs it, a world of possibilities opens up. The real work is helping people expand beyond it. Since 2014, I have been nurturing that curiosity for builders globally, guiding them from the first interaction to richer projects. AI is changing what a robot can be, letting it listen, converse and adapt to the people around it. I am applying for the Growth and Community Lead role at Furhat Robotics to grow those possibilities with the researchers and innovators shaping how people and machines interact.

Some of my favorite work has been helping people give robots a voice of their own. At Strawbees, I authored more than 100 platform resources, including coding and robotics guides, video tutorials and lesson plans. At international edtech conferences such as ISTE and EduTech Asia, I led talks and workshops on the programming potential of social and emotional robots, showing students, teachers and resellers how robots see, think, react and even replicate feelings.

I have spent almost a decade in hardware and software startups. I joined Strawbees as an early hire, built the educator community function from zero, and grew into Head of Education, wearing many hats across community, marketing, sales, product development and partnerships. I created products for new markets, such as a storybook series for South Korea and full digital courses for US go-to-market launches. I trained B2B resellers through webinars and trainings, and localized resources so they could lead activities at fairs and support their own customers. I also brought teacher feedback into our learning platform, and gave sales and marketing the stories and resources they needed for our technology to reach B2B school districts.

Researchers and innovation teams discover Furhat through conferences, research papers, colleagues, workshops and community conversations, often over months. I grew a community of 637 educators, hosted a monthly webinar series, and scaled an ambassador program across 10+ countries, training teachers to lead their own workshops, including at conferences, and bring in their colleagues. Training others is how a community grows beyond any one person, and at Furhat I would do the same, turning researchers into advocates and advocates into ambassadors who share what they build.

Furhat already gives people a clear path into building, from Blockly to the Remote API and Kotlin Skill API. For B2C community and B2B training alike, I design resources with low floors so users can start easily, high ceilings so they can grow into complex projects, and wide walls so they can follow their own interests. I have guided learners from block coding in Blockly, MakeCode and Scratch to text programming in Arduino, Processing and Python, creating step-by-step guides and code libraries that give them a clear next step. I am also a supportive presence: someone people feel comfortable asking for help, so that being stuck becomes part of learning, not a reason to give up. Every person who moves confidently from a first Blockly skill to a full study becomes someone who renews, recommends and returns.

I am based in Stockholm, and critical thinking, creativity and collaboration guide everything I build. I would love to talk about how I can help grow the Furhat community, and connect it to measurable growth alongside your marketing team.

Warm regards,

Lindsay Balfour
